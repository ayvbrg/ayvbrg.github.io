Teaching a diffusion model to ink like a human

11 Aug 2026 6 min read Ayushman Buragohain Updated 14 Aug 2026

devlogexperiment

There is a particular arrogance in asking a model that has only ever seen pixels to understand the pressure of a nib.

What the model missed

Ink is not colour. It is decision. A human inker commits: the line thickens where the form turns toward you and starves where it turns away. That commitment is the whole language, and it was the first thing the model refused to learn.

Three weeks later the real fix was duller and more human: a few hundred examples inked by hand, at two in the morning, until the model had something honest to imitate.
