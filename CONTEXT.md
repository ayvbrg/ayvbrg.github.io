# ayvbrg.log

Ayushman Buragohain's personal corner of the internet: a minimal, fast,
developer-focused site that is both a landing page and a personal log.

## Language

**Log**:
The site's writing section — the collection of all entries, indexed at `/log`.
_Avoid_: Blog, posts, articles (as section names)

**Entry**:
A single piece of writing in the log, authored as Markdown/MDX, living at
`/log/<slug>`.
_Avoid_: Post, article, blog post

**Tag**:
A label classifying an entry. The starting set is `devlog`, `experiment`,
`random`.
_Avoid_: Category

**Landing**:
The site's front page — introduces Ayushman and links to socials and work.
_Avoid_: Home, portfolio

**FAQ**:
A standalone personal-questions page (in the spirit of lilianweng.github.io/faq)
about Ayushman — what they work on, their setup, how to reach them.
_Avoid_: Uses, about (as separate concepts; the FAQ absorbs them)
